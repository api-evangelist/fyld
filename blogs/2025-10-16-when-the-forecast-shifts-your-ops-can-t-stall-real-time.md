Texas leads the United States in both the number and total cost of billion-dollar weather disasters. Since 1980, the state has experienced 190 such events, resulting in approximately $402 billion in cumulative damages . In 2023 alone, Texas saw 16 severe weather events, including floods, derechos, droughts, and freezes .
