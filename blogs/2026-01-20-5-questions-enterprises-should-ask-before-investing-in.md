Why do frontline technology decisions often fail? Frontline teams are vital for successful field operations across the critical infrastructure sector, yet enterprise technology strategies are often focused most on empowering desk-based workers. This disconnect is a major reason frontline technology investments struggle to scale or deliver long-term value.
