LONDON, UK, 12th March 2026: FYLD , the AI-powered, frontline intelligence platform for the global infrastructure sector, today announced a partnership with Amey , a leading infrastructure services provider, to deploy its technology across the company’s Highways division. The partnership is set to transform how more than 2,500 field workers plan, execute, and manage essential maintenance and improvement works.
